# Theory

## The problem

Teams may be unclear about:

- What goals and outcomes matter.
- Which problems or uncertainties to address.
- What work will help, how long it may take, and what success looks like.

When goals, work, decisions, and evidence are disconnected, it is harder to choose priorities, explain why work exists, or learn whether it helped.

## The idea

DevEnv provides practices, tools, and workflows to help teams work more effectively through a **Think–Do–Evaluate** loop:

- **Think:** clarify goals, investigate problems and uncertainties, and choose work with a reason.
- **Do:** carry out the work using suitable tools and workflows.
- **Evaluate:** compare evidence and results with intended outcomes, learn, and adapt.

The hypothesis is that clearer reasoning, better decisions, and connected work improve team efficiency and outcomes.

## Proving the value

DevEnv must use its own practices and tools to measure and demonstrate whether they help teams:

- Clarify goals, problems, and what success means.
- Choose and complete valuable work efficiently.
- Connect decisions and evidence to outcomes, identify gaps, and learn from results.

Evaluate these claims in real workflows against an observed baseline. Measure both efficiency and outcomes, including the effort required to use and maintain DevEnv. Treat benefits as unproven until supported by evidence.
